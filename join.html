Get Involved

Learn, serve, fundraise, build community, and support organ transplant awareness with OTHA.

Member Opportunities

Choose how you want to participate.

OTHA opportunities span outreach, fundraising, service, education, and social connection.

Outreach

Take part in speaker events, high-school presentations, campus awareness campaigns, and donor registration tabling.

Fundraising

Support transplant-related partners and programs through bake sales, merchandise, and collaborations with local businesses.

Service

Participate in health fairs, patient-support activities, transplant-community volunteering, and other hands-on service.

Member Meetings

Learn about transplantation, disparities, misconceptions, organ health, and practical ways students can help.

Special Opportunities

Sign up for our newsletter to receive information about research events, shadowing, volunteering, lab tours, and other special opportunities available to members.

Leadership
