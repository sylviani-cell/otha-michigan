About the Symposium

Transplant Center Research Symposium

The Transplant Center Research Symposium brings together physicians, researchers, healthcare professionals, students, and members of the transplant community to examine current challenges and innovations in transplantation.

Through keynote presentations, faculty speakers, and interdisciplinary discussions, the symposium explores organ-transplant access, biotechnology, organ availability and distribution, transplantation ethics, organ allocation, and socioeconomic, racial, and geographic disparities in patient outcomes.

OTHA members support the symposium by volunteering, helping with event setup and activities, tabling, and connecting students with the event.

Past symposiums

2025

1st Annual Transplant Center Research Symposium

Bridges to Life: Innovations in Transplant Access

The inaugural symposium focused on access in transplantation and brought together experts to discuss equity, organ availability, allocation, biotechnology, ethics, and disparities affecting patient outcomes.

2026

2nd Annual Transplant Center Research Symposium

Innovations in Transplantation: Beginning of a New Era

The second annual symposium highlighted innovations in artificial organs, xenotransplantation, organ-allocation policy, surgical techniques, machine perfusion, cardiac evaluation, and donor-derived infection management.
